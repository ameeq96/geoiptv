The comparison notebook

Choose with context.
Not just a price.

A practical guide to reading the GEO World IPTV catalogue and asking the questions that matter to your setup.

A useful comparison starts with what you actually need. Before choosing a Geo IPTV plan, write down your device, how many people may watch at once and the length of time you want to commit. Those details make the options easier to judge.

Put the whole term on the same scale

Compare the 1-, 3-, 6- and 12-month options by looking at the total term price first. To work out a monthly equivalent, divide that total by the number of months. This is a comparison tool, not a promise of monthly billing. Check the amount due at checkout, including any charges shown there, and compare the same connection allowance. A longer term is not automatically better value for your circumstances.

Separate devices from simultaneous connections

A list of supported devices does not tell you how many screens can play at the same time. Ask about the connection limit for the exact plan you are considering. Share your television or device model, operating system and preferred player with support. If you expect to switch between a television and a phone, ask how that works before paying rather than assuming both can play together.

Make your viewing checklist specific

If a particular language or channel matters to you, ask support to confirm it for the selected plan. Give the exact name and explain where you intend to use the service. A broad catalogue label is not confirmation of every language, channel or location. Keep the reply alongside your chosen plan details, and review any access requirements and renewal terms before completing checkout. The aim is an informed choice, not simply the longest subscription.

Is a monthly equivalent the amount charged each month?

Not necessarily. It spreads a term price over its duration so you can compare options. Use the selected plan and checkout summary to confirm the actual amount and payment schedule. Ask support if either is unclear.

What should I confirm before choosing a longer plan?

Confirm the device and player setup, simultaneous connection limit, required languages or channels, and when access begins and ends. Read the applicable renewal and refund terms as well. Talk through any unanswered questions with support before committing.
